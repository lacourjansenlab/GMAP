############
Print Colors
############

When printing something in GMAP, you can do so with colors. By default, GMAP works with 24-bit colors. To support older terminals/command lines, colors are automatially converted to 4-bit when requested by the user. The following images show what 24-bit colors are converted to what 4-bit ones, to pick the best one.

As colors take up a 3-dimensional space, it is hard to visualize in 2D what happens. Therefore, the cube has been sliced along all three directions. Use the menu on the right to pick the overview that works for you!
